# Day 3 - Role Based Prompting

## What is Role-Based Prompting?

Role-Based Prompting means assigning a specific role to the AI before asking a question. This helps the AI generate more focused and relevant responses.

---

## Without Role

### Prompt:
Explain Artificial Intelligence.

### Output:
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, problem-solving, decision-making, language understanding, and pattern recognition. AI is used in applications such as virtual assistants, recommendation systems, self-driving cars, and chatbots.

---

## Persona 1 - MBA Professor

### Prompt:
You are an MBA professor.

Explain Artificial Intelligence in simple words for MBA students.

### Output:
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that helps businesses make smarter decisions by analyzing large amounts of data quickly and accurately. Companies use AI for customer service, marketing, sales forecasting, fraud detection, and process automation. AI improves efficiency, reduces costs, and helps organizations gain a competitive advantage. For MBA students, understanding AI is important because it is transforming the way modern businesses operate.

---

## Persona 2 - Software Engineer

### Prompt:
You are a software engineer.

Explain Artificial Intelligence from a technical perspective.

### Output:
Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the development of computer systems that can simulate human intelligence. It involves technologies such as Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Computer Vision. AI systems are trained using data and algorithms to recognize patterns, make predictions, and perform tasks automatically. Software engineers build AI models using programming languages like Python and frameworks such as TensorFlow and PyTorch.

---

## Comparison

| Aspect | Without Role | MBA Professor | Software Engineer |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Focus | General explanation | Business applications | Technical concepts |
| Audience | Everyone | MBA students | Developers and engineers |
| Style | Informative | Business-oriented | Technical and detailed |
