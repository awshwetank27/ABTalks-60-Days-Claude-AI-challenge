# Day 2 - Prompt Engineering

## Before (Weak Prompt)

Tell me about AI.

## After (Engineered Prompt)

Act as an AI mentor.

Explain Artificial Intelligence to a beginner in simple language.

Include:
- Definition
- 3 real-world examples
- Benefits
- Future career opportunities

Keep the explanation under 300 words.

## What I Learned

A structured prompt gives better and more accurate results than a vague prompt.
